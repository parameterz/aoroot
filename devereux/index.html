This address used to hold a stand-alone worksheet for Devereux et al., Am J Cardiol 2012: mean and upper limits by age, from sex and BSA or height. It has been retired.

Devereux 2012 is still here

now with company

The calculator scores a measurement against Devereux 2012 and several newer adult references side by side, so you can see where they agree and where they don't. For each reference you get:

  • a z-score, percent of predicted and normal range for the patient's own age, sex and body size, instead of a table of limits across every age;
  • notes on the reference population and how the study measured the root;
  • for Devereux, an option to apply a corrected standard error, with an explanation of why.

Every Devereux coefficient, and the table it came from, is listed on the references page.
